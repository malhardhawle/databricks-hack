Table catalog

Eight tables. volumetrics is the core fact table. The dimension tables are derived from it or from ngl_volumes, and their pre-computed totals need care (see Data quality).

Provincial production

volumetrics where ActivityID = 'PROD'. Every PROD row is well-level (FromToIDType = 'WI'). Volumes are converted to daily rates so that short months like February don't look like dips.

Oil

bbl/d · m³ × 6.2898 ÷ days

Gas

MMcf/d · e³m³ × 35.3147 ÷ days. Seasonal winter peaks

Condensate

bbl/d. Strongest growth of any product

Produced water

m³/d. About 4.5× oil by volume

What the rows are

One facility-month spawns many rows: production, then disposition, receipt downstream, inventory, fuel and so on. That's why summing Volume across activities double-counts.
